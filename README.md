# Isle-fly

## Trolling Lure Design Specification

### Overview

This document describes the design of an offshore skirted trolling lure intended for targeting large pelagic species such as tuna, mahi-mahi, wahoo, and marlin.

---

### Lure Profile

| Property        | Specification                          |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Style**       | Skirted offshore trolling lure         |
| **Head Length** | 5 inches (127 mm)                      |
| **Overall Length (with skirt)** | 10–12 inches (254–305 mm) |
| **Head Diameter (nose)** | 1.75 inches (44 mm)          |
| **Head Diameter (collar)** | 1.25 inches (32 mm)         |
| **Weight**      | 3–4 oz (85–113 g)                      |
| **Running Depth** | Surface to 6 ft (0–1.8 m)           |
| **Target Speed**| 6–9 knots                              |

---

### Head Design

- **Shape:** Bullet/cone-shaped slant-face head with a concave angled face (approximately 30° from vertical) to create turbulence and bubble trail.
- **Material:** High-density acrylic or resin cast, UV-resistant.
- **Finish:** Gloss clear coat over painted base.
- **Eyes:** 3D holographic inserted eyes, 12 mm diameter, placed 0.75 inches back from the nose face.
- **Color Scheme (primary):** Blue/purple back fade to silver belly with pink highlights — a classic "candy" pattern visible at depth.
- **Through-wire:** Stainless steel 49-strand wire, 480 lb (217 kg) break strength, runs full length of head and loops at nose and collar.

### Skirt Design

- **Material:** Soft vinyl with holographic or mylar flash strands interwoven.
- **Number of layers:** 2 (outer and inner skirt).
- **Outer skirt:** 8-inch squid-style tentacles, solid or translucent color matching head.
- **Inner skirt:** 6-inch shorter tentacles, contrasting flash color (silver or gold mylar).
- **Collar attachment:** Snug-fit over head collar with stainless steel band clamp or heat-shrink tubing.

---

### Hook Rig

- **Configuration:** Tandem hook rig (lead hook + trailing stinger hook).
- **Lead hook:** 9/0 Mustad or equivalent stainless steel, offset point.
- **Trailing hook:** 8/0, connected via 6-inch 480 lb mono or wire bridle.
- **Hook position:** Trailing hook rests at rear edge of skirt.
- **Leader material:** 200 lb monofilament or fluorocarbon, 6 ft minimum.

---

### Action & Rigging Notes

- Designed to swim just below the surface, creating a bubble trail and side-to-side "swimming" action at trolling speed.
- Best deployed from the long rigger or flat line position, 30–80 feet behind the transom.
- Rig on the center rigger for rougher sea states; the slant face helps keep the lure on the surface in chop.
- Replace skirt every 3–5 trips or when vinyl shows cracking or fading.
